An original, breathtaking solarpunk landscape for Yavin, a Brazilian research community. A cinematic wide establishing shot from a shaded botanical observation terrace, looking into a lush tropical valley in warm early morning sunlight. A small ambitious community weaves between gardens, reflecting water and immense trees. Inventive low-rise workshops and communal buildings: terracotta walls, pale curved structural frames, deep verandas, wood, sun-catching roofs, a distinctive hexagonal scientific station with a glass skylight slightly right of center. Winding footpaths and little bridges connect buildings and garden terraces. A few tiny people and charming friendly utilitarian robots garden, tend equipment and walk together. Technology is resourceful, tactile, playful and integrated into daily life.
ART DIRECTION: expressive hand-drawn 2D animation background painting, confident ink contours with varied line weight, strong designed silhouettes, rich painterly gouache textures, layered tropical foliage, atmospheric perspective. Fresh, bold, sophisticated and warm. Beautiful imaginative mechanical object design. Original visual identity, not a reproduction of an existing film, no photorealism and no 3D render.
COMPOSITION: panoramic 16:9 with strong foreground framing, middle distance and expansive distant horizon. Large shaded tree canopy and dark botanical mass at upper-left provide a quiet dark area behind cream typography that will be added later. Sunlit world opens across the center and right. Lower central foreground is a relatively calm terrace for a small interactive observation instrument to be added separately. The path invites the viewer toward the station. Important scene details in the central area must survive a tall mobile crop. Foreground plants frame without blocking the view.
MOOD AND PALETTE: a world cared for, still being built. Warm gold sunlight and soft luminous clouds, deep cool botanical green shade, blue-green distant hills, honey cream, coral terracotta, aged brass and timber. Quiet breeze, optimism, wonder, believable human scale.
Landscape artwork only. NO text, NO lettering, NO logos, NO watermark, NO border, NO UI. Do not paint an observation instrument, button or visor. No generic glass skyscrapers, giant city skyline, cyberpunk neon, dystopia or glossy 3D plastic.
