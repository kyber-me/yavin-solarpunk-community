Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: Full-screen illustrated landscape for the arrival experience of Yavin, a Brazilian solarpunk research community.
Primary request: An original, breathtaking, inviting future landscape, seen from a shaded botanical observation terrace. It must feel like a place one longs to enter, full of human warmth, inventiveness and life.
Scene: A lush tropical valley in warm morning light. A small, ambitious community weaves between gardens, water and trees. Distinctive low-rise workshops and communal buildings, terracotta surfaces, pale structural frames, deep verandas, sun-catching roofs and a hexagonal scientific station with a skylight. Footpaths connect the buildings. Solar technology feels integrated, practical and playful. A few tiny people and friendly utilitarian robots are gardening, tending equipment and walking together, as part of daily life.
Style/medium: Expressive hand-drawn 2D animation background painting, confident ink contours of varied weight, beautifully designed silhouettes, painterly gouache textures, layered foliage and atmospheric perspective. Fresh, bold, sophisticated illustration with charming, inventive object design. Not photorealistic, not a 3D render. Create an original artistic identity rather than reproducing a frame or character from an existing film.
Composition: Cinematic wide landscape, 16:9. Strong foreground, middle distance and distant horizon. The eye follows a winding path toward the scientific station, slightly right of center. Shade and a framing tree canopy at the upper-left make room for cream typography added separately in the interface. The lower central area stays relatively calm for a small interactive observation instrument added separately. Interesting central composition must also survive a tall mobile crop. Foreground plants and terrace edges frame the view without blocking it. A sense of scale and an expansive, hopeful horizon.
Lighting/mood: Warm sunlight, soft golden clouds, deep cool green shade, sparkling water, quiet morning breeze, optimistic and alive. A world cared for, still being built.
Palette/materials: Botanical greens, blue-green distant hills, warm cream, honey sunlight, coral terracotta, aged brass and timber accents.
Text: None.
Constraints: Landscape only, no UI, no lettering, no logo, no watermark, no border. Do not paint the observation instrument or CTA into the image; those are interactive interface elements. No giant city skyline, no generic glass skyscrapers, no neon cyberpunk, no dystopia. A future imagined, not a factual depiction of the current project or a choice of real-world location.
