Use case: ui-mockup. Create one high quality landscape 16:10 concept sketch / visual prototype for the entrance screen of YAVIN, a Brazilian solarpunk research community web application. This is a proposed visual direction, not a city already built. Show the actual flat desktop application screen edge to edge, no laptop, no phone, no external presentation frame. Composition: slim very dark green header with refined small wordmark 'YAVIN' on left and small label 'Estudo visual · 01' at right. The remaining screen is a quiet deep forest teal near-black field containing ONE beautifully illustrated axonometric 2.5D solarpunk laboratory building on a small irregular patch of earth and vegetation. Make the laboratory the large central focal point, entirely visible, ample breathing room and readable silhouette. No other buildings, no city, no roads grid, no menu, no sidebar and NO CHAT on the entrance screen. At bottom center below the building, an elegant restrained place label 'Laboratório' in a literary serif font, small sans-serif caption 'Um espaço para investigar Yavin.' and a simple green button 'Entrar no laboratório' clearly suggesting this opens a separate conversation page. Art direction: hand-drawn detailed comic / ligne-claire-inspired illustration, expressive fine dark outlines, simplified tactile volumes, flat color shapes with selective painted texture and block shading, NO pixel art, NO low-poly 3D render, NO photorealism, NO glossy mobile game toy. Elevated fixed axonometric camera looking down at two visible faces and roof, same scale and coherent perspective. Building: a modest welcoming two-storey community research atelier, believable human scale. Warm pale earth plaster, exposed timber, curved veranda canopy and broad eaves, a few organic Art Nouveau structural curves used sparingly. Integrate 2-3 clearly legible blue photovoltaic panels, small rainwater cistern with visible pipe, planted roof sections, broad-leaf tropical plants, vines, a little shared workbench with research notebooks visible under the covered veranda. One clear accessible front entry facing viewer, warm amber light in workshop windows. Convey useful lived-in craft, ecological infrastructure, cooperative investigation and gradual construction. Do not overload the building. Colors: foliage varied deep and bright moss greens, ochre wood and warm clay, muted turquoise glass and blue solar cells, small warm amber window highlights, all stand out against calm very dark teal background. Lighting: optimistic gentle daylight on the illustrated lab and its plants, not a gloomy night scene, despite dark UI. The ground patch should blend artistically at edges into background, not a floating cubic game tile. Landscape composition should make the lab look like a single explorable map location. Clear beautiful Portuguese typography, ONLY the exact labels specified, no invented gibberish or signage. No decorative annotations, no watermarks, no extra controls.
