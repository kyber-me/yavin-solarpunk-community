# Jugaad

## Definition

**Jugaad** (also *jugaar* or *jugāṛ*) is a term used in [South Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Asia) for improvising a solution with the resources at hand. It can refer to the resourcefulness involved or to the resulting object. The term is written जुगाड़ in [Hindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindi), جگاڑ in [Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu), and ਜੁਗਾੜ in [Punjabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjabi_language). [Wiktionary entry](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/jugaad) · [Punjabi–English dictionary](https://dic.learnpunjabi.org/default.asp).

## Uses and interpretations

In management, *jugaad* is associated with [frugal innovation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frugal_innovation) and finding solutions under tight budgets and limited resources. Wikipedia mentions applications in [research and development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_and_development) and draws a parallel with [life hacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_hack). [Review of frugal innovation in business](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11662995) · [*Jugaad Innovation* by Navi Radjou, Jaideep Prabhu, and Simone Ahuja](https://www.penguin.co.in/book/jugaad-innovation/).

Research on everyday infrastructure examines how designers and users adapt, combine, and repair components to keep systems working or put them to new uses. [Royal Geographical Society](https://www.rgs.org/about-us/our-work/latest-news/research-spotlight-jugaad-infrastructure-improvisational-infrastructures-in-everyday-life/) · [Ankit Kumar, *Jugaad Infrastructure*](https://doi.org/10.1002%2Fgeo2.153).

In 2013, [Jaideep Prabhu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaideep_Prabhu) argued that the practice had lessons for both developed and emerging economies. [Hindustan Times interview](http://www.hindustantimes.com/india/cambridge-expert-says-indian-jugaad-is-lesson-to-world/story-M3q6vFu8tEPj8eSNsAJo5M.html).

## Improvised vehicles

![Motorcycle towing another with a rope](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c3/Jugaad_technology_%28A_motorcycle_pulling_another_one_by_a_rope%29.jpg/250px-Jugaad_technology_%28A_motorcycle_pulling_another_one_by_a_rope%29.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)

Motorcycle towing another with a rope. psubhashish, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0). [File page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Jugaad_technology_%28A_motorcycle_pulling_another_one_by_a_rope%29.jpg).

In [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India), [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh), and [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan), *jugaad* can also refer to vehicles that local mechanics build from wood, sheet metal, and salvaged parts. In [northern India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_India), the [peter rehra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_rehra) is a type of [quadricycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadricycle) that can reuse [SUV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SUV) parts and a diesel engine from an [irrigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrigation) pump. [Livemint](http://www.livemint.com/Companies/Bt7C5EnNrvRyoqZAERSbMN/Rural-India8217s-jugaad-for-cheap-travel.html).

### Safety and registration

Wikipedia describes braking problems and cites reports from 2010 and 2015 about vehicle seizures and difficulties registering these vehicles under Indian law. Brake reliability and the loads these vehicles carry are two factors to examine when assessing their safety. [Law cited by Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motor_Vehicles_Act,_1988) · [The Indian Express](http://indianexpress.com/article/india/india-news-india/one-hack-of-a-vehicle/) · [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/jaipur/Govt-issues-order-to-seize-jugaads/articleshow/5707131.cms).

The article mentions a study by Thomas Birtchnell of the [University of Wollongong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wollongong), based on records from [J. N. Medical College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jawaharlal_Nehru_Medical_College,_Aligarh) in [Aligarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aligarh,_Uttar_Pradesh). Its discussion of registration cites [Pon Radhakrishnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pon_Radhakrishnan), then a minister at the [Ministry of Road Transport and Highways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Road_Transport_and_Highways), and covers [licence plates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plate), [regional transport offices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_Transport_Office), and [road tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_tax).

### Regional variants

| Variant | Region and features |
| --- | --- |
| *Bike-rehra* or *motorcycle-rehri* | A motorcycle, moped, or scooter converted into a [motorised tricycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motorized_tricycle), with examples in northern India, particularly in [Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punjab,_India). | *Phat-phatri* or *phatphatiya* | A [motorised rickshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auto_rickshaw) once used in New Delhi, built from [Harley-Davidson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harley_Davidson) motorcycles dating to the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWII). [Heritage Transport Museum](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/phat-phat-a-reincarnation-of-harley-davidson-heritage-transport-museum/HQXBdYEWWlDUIg?hl=en) |
| *Meen badi vandi* | A cargo tricycle used to carry fish in [Tamil Nadu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_Nadu), [southern India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_India). Motorised versions reuse engines from motorcycles such as [Yezdi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yezdi) and [Enfield Bullet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Enfield_Bullet). [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/chennai/also-ran-when-a-city-threw-together-a-fish-cart/article67213765.ece) |
| *Chakkda* | A motorcycle converted into a tricycle with truck wheels on the rear axle, documented in [Gujarat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gujarat). [Be On The Road](https://www.beontheroad.com/2018/02/the-chakda-indias-true-jugaad-vehicle.html#) |
| *Chand-gari* or *chingchee* | A motorcycle-based rickshaw used in [Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pakistan). The name *chingchee* comes from the manufacturer [Jinan Qingqi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinan_Qingqi). [The News International](https://www.thenews.com.pk/print/925391-motorcycle-rickshaws-to-be-replaced-with-electric-bikes) |

## Related concepts in Yavin

- **Solarpunk**. Examples of *jugaad* can inform discussions about how communities maintain and repair infrastructure with local resources. [Yavin founding minutes in Portuguese](../atas/ATA_001_Fundacao_de_Yavin.md).
- **Low-tech**. Reusing components and repairing equipment locally are points of overlap. The tools required and the conditions of use help explain why an improvised solution works in a given setting.
- **Swadeshi**. Local autonomy is a point of comparison. A separate entry can explain the role of local production in Swadeshi, while this entry covers how people improvise with available resources.

For retrieval in Yavin, **Definition**, **Uses and interpretations**, and **Improvised vehicles** can each be read independently. Follow the source links when a question needs more detail.
